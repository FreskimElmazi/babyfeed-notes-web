Delete Your Account

Baby Feed - Notes

📱 Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Baby Feed - Notes and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. If asked, sign in again and repeat.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

✉️ Request deletion by email

No longer have the app? Email support@babyfeednotes.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

🗑️ What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login)
  • Your profile, settings and notification token
  • Babies you created alone, with all their sleep, feeding and diaper entries
  • Your membership in a shared family

📦 What is kept

  • Shared babies: if you share a baby with a partner, that baby and its entries stay available to your partner. You are removed from it.
  • Purchase records: subscription history is kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) as required for billing and tax purposes. Cancel an active subscription in your App Store or Google Play settings — deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (only if you allowed them) are not linked to your account and are removed by Google Analytics automatically when its retention period ends (at most 14 months).
